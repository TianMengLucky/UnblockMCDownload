## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: landscape anime wallpaper, also the background of the UnblockMCDownload desktop application. Create ONE finished 16:10 wide wallpaper, approximately 1920x1200 or 2048x1280, with an opaque painted background.
Input reference roles: image 1 is the identity reference for the white-haired dragon girl. Images 2, 3 and 4 are three separate software-logo identity references to be transformed into THREE different cute anthropomorphized chibi companions. Make exactly FOUR characters total, all matching a cute 2-heads-tall Japanese anime chibi illustration style.
Scene: a peaceful sunlit grassland with a winding little footpath, soft rolling green hills, tiny wildflowers, a distant voxel-inspired grove, a soft blue sky, fluffy clouds and warm gentle sunlight. The four friends are taking a relaxed walk together on the grass, smiling and chatting. Show full bodies and walking poses, friendly companionship, no fighting and no locks in this wallpaper.
Character 1: the girl from reference 1, recognizable long snow-white hair with pale lavender shading, curved ivory dragon horns, large purple eyes, pointed ears, small pale lavender dragon wings and a curled scaled dragon tail, and a simplified modest white-and-lavender fantasy dress with floral ornaments. Make her cheerful and very cute.
Character 2: anthropomorphized PCL mascot from reference 2. A cute chibi humanlike adventurer with short deep-blue hair, bright blue eyes, a blue-and-navy geometric adventurer outfit, and a clearly recognizable blue HEXAGONAL PCL badge on the chest or a small hexagonal satchel. Personality calm and helpful. Preserve the hexagon visual identity. The badge may say the short letters "PCL", kept small and secondary.
Character 3: anthropomorphized PCL-CE mascot from reference 3. A DIFFERENT cute chibi humanlike adventurer with lighter cyan-blue hair, a square hair ornament and a royal-blue / cyan jacket with SQUARE angular trim. A blue SQUARE badge or shoulder bag may carry the short letters "CE". Personality energetic and playful. Make the character visibly distinct from PCL, especially the square motif versus the hexagon motif; do not merge them into one person.
Character 4: anthropomorphized HMCL mascot from reference 4. A cute chibi craftsperson with warm brown hair, a small crafting-table-pattern hat or hair clip, a cream and earthy-brown outfit with a tiny apron and golden-brown accents, and a crafting-grid motif on the satchel. Friendly, inventive personality. Preserve the crafting-table brown/cream visual identity.
Composition for application usability: the four characters form one compact group occupying the RIGHT 48% of the image and the lower-right middle ground, with clearly visible faces and walking feet. The LEFT 45% should be comparatively simple, softly painted green meadow and pale sky with a quiet low-detail area where application controls can be overlaid later. Keep a consistent natural landscape across the whole image, not a split panel. Keep the important characters away from the extreme right edge and leave headroom at top for a small app title bar. Horizon in the upper third. Character group should be large enough to enjoy as wallpaper, not tiny distant figures. Full scene bleeds to all edges, no sticker borders, no white cutout outline, no rounded frame.
Style: polished bright 2D anime key visual, soft cel shading, clean expressive linework, warm pastel greens and blues balanced with lavender, lively glossy eyes, gentle flower and grass details. Cute and wholesome. No photorealism, no 3D render, no large typography, no title, no watermark, no UI screenshot, no extra characters. Small identity emblems on clothing are the only allowed lettering.
```
