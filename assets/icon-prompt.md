# 图标生成记录

生成方式：内置 `image_gen`，透明背景。

参考图一用于人物设定，参考图二用于 Q 版二次元画风。原始输出保存在 `icon.png`；`icon-256.png` 和多尺寸 `icon.ico` 是缩放及格式转换产物。

## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: illustration-story / application icon.
Create ONE polished square application icon for UnblockMCDownload, with a genuinely transparent background. The first input image is the CHARACTER IDENTITY reference. The second input image is ONLY the CHIBI ANIME DRAWING STYLE reference, not its characters or its exact composition.
Main scene: the white-haired dragon girl from image 1 is cheerfully unlocking a locked Minecraft grass-block icon with a small golden key. Show one large recognizable voxel grass-and-dirt cube, a golden padlock attached across its front by a simple strap, and the girl turning the key in the lock. The padlock shackle has visibly sprung OPEN: this is clearly an unlocking / access-restored action. A tiny sparkle at the open shackle reinforces the action.
Keep the girl's recognizable identity: very long snow-white hair with faint lavender shading, two curved ivory dragon horns, pointed ears, large lavender-purple eyes, pale lavender batlike dragon wings, a curled pale lavender scaled dragon tail, and a modest white-and-lavender fantasy dress with simplified floral ornaments. Translate her into a super-cute 2-head-tall chibi with a large expressive face, one playful wink, rosy cheeks, short limbs, and tiny hands. ONE girl only. She leans affectionately over / beside the grass block to reach the lock, her face, key, and open padlock all easy to see. Simplify the flowing dress rather than making a long full-height portrait.
Match the lively 2D anime sticker rendering language of reference 2: crisp dark outlines, expressive cute face, rich cel shading, vibrant yet balanced colors, glossy eye highlights, a clean thick white sticker border and a thin darker outer contour so the silhouette reads on both dark and light UI. Character colors stay white, silver and lavender; cube colors grassy green and earthy brown; lock and key warm gold.
Composition: a compact centered single emblem, square 1:1, approximately 1024x1024, subject fills about 88% of canvas with comfortable transparent padding. Keep hair, horns, wings, tail and block within frame. Distinct silhouettes and large shapes readable at 64px. The open padlock is an important large visual feature, not a tiny background detail.
No text, no lettering, no watermark, no app mockup, no opaque backdrop, no decorative frame, no additional characters. Do not use the blue-haired or red-haired characters from image 2.
```
